A deal team where every role has one job

Each role is a skill with its own checklist and a fixed report. Agents build and check; only you approve the export.

  1. Agents/analyst

    Builds the workbook from cited data, after agreeing the assumptions with you.

  2. Agents/associate

    Ties every typed number to the filing, cell by cell, and flags what doesn't agree.

  3. Agents/vp-review

    Runs the deterministic model checks, then reviews the assumptions against history.

  4. Agents/md-redteam

    Argues against the valuation: what moves it, and what the market implies.

  5. Agents/compliance

    Checks the disclaimer, the sources and the language before anything is shared.

  6. You/sign-off

    Shows every open item, and exports a signed copy only after you approve in your own words.

Reviewers report; they never edit your file. In Claude Code and Cowork they run as separate agents that don't see how the model was built. They also review workbooks made with Anthropic's financial-analysis plugin.

The benchmark

Claude Sonnet 5.5 built a DCF for each of 50 companies (35 US, 15 European), three ways. Every reported figure was scored against the filing, and every one flagged as wrong was read by hand.

Accuracy, checked every night

Golden questions answered exactly as filed, on every change: US and European companies, a bank, restatements, a stock split, fiscal years ending in January, March, June and September. Each night, the answers are re-read from the filings with independent parsers.

Install in three steps

No terminal, no account to create. You need a Claude account with code execution on; the roles need a paid plan.

  1. Add the data connector

    In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste the URL, with no sign-in.

    Coming soon

    The hosted connector isn't online yet. You can run your own copy for free in a few minutes: self-hosting guide.

  2. Add the deal team

    Open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace. Paste this repository, then install deskstack.

    https://github.com/joaquimmj/deskstack

  3. Ask for a model

    Claude builds the DCF, has every number checked and reviewed, then waits for your approval.

    /deal-team Apple quick-dcf

What it doesn't do

  • No share prices and no consensus estimates: a market cap needs a price you provide.
  • European coverage is partial and annual only; German issuers aren't in the public repository yet.
  • Banks and insurers are flagged: a standard DCF doesn't fit them.
  • Reviews reduce errors but don't replace a human reviewer. Everything is a draft until you sign it off.
