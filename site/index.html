Every number in your model, tied out to the filing.

deskstack gives Claude free financial data with a source on every figure, and a review team. One agent builds the model, others check each number against the SEC or European filing, and nothing is exported until you sign it off.

Install in Claude Read the tutorial

Install in three steps

No terminal, no account to create. You need a Claude account with code execution on; the roles need a paid plan.

  1. Add the data connector

    In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste the URL, with no sign-in.

    Coming soon

    The hosted connector isn't online yet. You can run your own copy for free in a few minutes: self-hosting guide.

  2. Add the deal team

    Open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace. Paste this repository, then install deskstack.

    https://github.com/joaquimmj/deskstack

  3. Ask for a model

    Claude builds the DCF, has every number checked and reviewed, then waits for your approval.

    /deal-team Apple quick-dcf

A team where every role has one job

Each role is a skill with its own checklist and a fixed report. They run in this order, and you can call any of them on its own, on any workbook.

  1. /analyst

    Builds the workbook from cited data, after agreeing the assumptions with you.

  2. /associate

    Checks every typed number against the filing and says why one doesn't agree.

  3. /vp-review

    Runs 15 model checks, then compares the assumptions with filed history.

  4. /md-redteam

    Argues against the valuation and shows what the market price implies.

  5. /compliance

    Checks the disclaimer, the citations and signs of non-public information.

  6. /sign-off

    Shows every open item and exports only after you approve in your own words.

Reviewers report; they never edit your file. In Claude Code and Cowork they run as separate agents that don't see how the model was built. They also review workbooks made with Anthropic's financial-analysis plugin.

How we know the numbers are right

Accuracy

100/100

Golden questions answered exactly as filed, on every change: US and European companies, a bank, restatements, a stock split, fiscal years ending in January, March, June and September. Each night, the answers are re-read from the filings with independent parsers.

How the golden set works

Benchmark

We measure how many numbers are wrong or unsourced when Claude builds a model with web search only, with deskstack's data, and with deskstack's reviewers too: 50 companies, a DCF and a comps table each, scored automatically against the filings. A first pilot ran on 5 companies. It gave no headline number: most of the figures scored as wrong for web search alone turned out to be definition differences or gaps in deskstack's own data, fixed since. The scoring now tells those apart; the full run is next. Every transcript is public, including where deskstack doesn't help.

The method and the company set

Free data, with the source on every figure

Official filings and public datasets only. Missing figures stay empty; they are never estimated.

What it doesn't do

  • No share prices and no consensus estimates: a market cap needs a price you provide.
  • European coverage is partial and annual only; German issuers aren't in the public repository yet.
  • Banks and insurers are flagged: a standard DCF doesn't fit them.
  • Reviews reduce errors but don't replace a human reviewer. Everything is a draft until you sign it off.
