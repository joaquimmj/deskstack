What web search got wrong

In the DCFs, numbers it couldn't find and filled in anyway: derived share counts, estimated capex, non-GAAP revenue. In the comps, twelve-month figures a quarter out of date, from an aggregator.

With more searches?

A pre-registered re-run with 30 searches and high effort still wrote a figure that isn't the filing's. The placeholders went away; the cost per table more than doubled.

Robustness check

It checks us too

The runs found gaps in deskstack's own data and two bugs in the scorer. All fixed, tested, and written up with the results.

Without Claude

Check any AI-built model in one command.

deskstack-check checks any .xlsx model, whether Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, another tool or a person built it. Offline, it runs the 21 model checks. With --verify, it also ties every typed figure to the filing; only those figures are sent.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Is it free?

Yes. The data connector runs on Cloudflare's free plan with a daily cap shared by everyone, and the code is MIT-licensed. For heavy use, deploy your own copy in a few minutes: self-hosting guide.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the filings themselves: SEC EDGAR for US companies, ESEF reports on filings.xbrl.org for European ones, plus the U.S. Treasury, the ECB and Damodaran's datasets. Every figure carries its source.

Does the server see my workbook?

No. The review scripts run in your own Claude session. The server only sees the values the associate sends to verify_value, and keeps no user data. Privacy.

What doesn't it do?

No share prices and no consensus estimates: a market cap needs a price you provide. European coverage is partial and annual only. Banks and insurers are flagged, since a standard DCF doesn't fit them.

Does it replace a reviewer?

No. It catches what can be checked: figures against filings, formulas against rules, language against a checklist. Everything is a draft until you sign it off.

Is it made by Anthropic?

No. deskstack is an independent open-source project that works with Claude. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
